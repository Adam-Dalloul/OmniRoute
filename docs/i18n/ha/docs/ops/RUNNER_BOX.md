Rukunin self-hosted yana aiki a kan na’urar **.113** (`tomni-proxmox-113`). Tun daga 2026-10-18, kowane
runner na OmniRoute yana da **rawar aiki guda ɗaya**, alama guda ɗaya, da kasafin ƙwaƙwalwa/CPU da aka daidaita da abin da
aka auna aikinsa na buƙata. Gurbin Turbopack yana samun abin da aikin ginawa ke buƙata; sauran guraben kuwa an daidaita su da
kololuwar amfani da aka auna. Na’urar tana ware fiye da ƙarfinta ne kawai idan aikin gina Turbopack ya zo lokaci guda da aikin gina
webpack na Release-Green da aikin lint; swap na na’urar mai masauki (16 GB) yana ɗaukar wannan, kuma saboda ayyukan gina na
pull request suna gudana a kan runners na waje, gurbin Turbopack ba ya aiki a mafi yawan lokaci:

| runner               | alama          | rawa                                                                                                                                                         | `MemoryHigh` / `MemoryMax` | `CPUQuota`    |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | ------------- |
| `omniroute-113-5`    | `omni-build`   | Ayyukan gina Turbopack: `ci.yml` `Build` (push/dispatch kawai), Docker amd64, `npm-publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`, aikin gina a11y na dare | 56 GB / 60 GB (swap 0)     | 1600 %        |
| `omniroute-113-6`    | `omni-release` | Release-Green (`release-green` + `main-green`), aikin gina webpack                                                                                           | 20 GB / 24 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 800 %         |
| `omniroute-113-2`    | `omni-light`   | ayyukan dare (Schemathesis, promptfoo, garak, k6, fara a11y) da `quality.yml` `lint-guard`                                                                   | 12 GB / 16 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 600 %         |
| `omniroute-113`      | `omni-light`   | rukuni ɗaya da na sama                                                                                                                                       | 8 GB / 12 GB               | 400 %         |
| OmniHeuris, OmniMind | (repos ɗinsu)  | Dependabot kawai (kololuwar 155 MB kowannensu, 2026-10-18)                                                                                                   | 4 GB / 6 GB                | 200 % kowanne |

An auna a 2026-10-18:

| albarkatu | ƙima                        | abin da hakan ke nufi ga tsara jadawalin aiki                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RAM / CPU | **78 GB / ƙwayoyin CPU 32** | aikin gina production na Turbopack ya kai kololuwar 49 GB ba tare da iyaka ba (`tests/unit/ci-build-bundler-webpack.test.ts`); a Next 16.3.8, matakin haɗawa kaɗai yana riƙe da ~40 GB (36 GB RSS a cikin `next-build` tare da ma’aikatan loader 17 a 3 GB, an auna a 2026-10-18); aikin gina webpack yana amfani da ~14–16 GB. Aikin Turbopack ɗaya + aikin webpack ɗaya za su iya gudana tare; ayyukan Turbopack biyu ba za su iya ba |
| swap      | 16 GB                       | `omniroute-113-5` yana aiki tare da kashe swap (`MemorySwapMax=0`) domin a dakatar da aikin gina da ya fita daga iko a iyakarsa maimakon ya sa na’urar ta fara amfani da swap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `/tmp`    | **12 GB tmpfs = RAM**       | duk abin da aka ajiye a wurin yana amfani da ƙwaƙwalwa; ana share ragowar bayan awa 3                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| CPU       | an ga load 60 a ƙwayoyi 32  | kafin saita ƙayyadaddun iyaka, aikin gina Turbopack ɗaya (~1166 % CPU) tare da jerin gwaje-gwajen coverage a kan na’ura ɗaya sun hana siginar bugun zuciyar sauran masu sauraro samun CPU mai isa (`The self-hosted runner lost communication`, #16853)                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| runners   | **masu sauraro 6**          | OmniRoute 4 + OmniHeuris + OmniMind; an kashe `omniroute-113-3/-4/-7/-8` (`systemctl enable --now` yana dawo da ɗaya aiki)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Ƙayyadaddun iyakokin suna cikin `/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.<…>.service.d/override.conf` na kowace unit
(an adana ƙimomin da suka gabata a matsayin `override.conf.bak-20261018`) kuma an aiwatar da su kai tsaye ta hanyar
`systemctl set-property --runtime … MemoryHigh=… MemoryMax=… CPUQuota=…` — ba tare da sake farawa ba, don haka ba a
taɓa wani aiki da ke gudana ba. Ana canza alamomi ta API na runners
(`gh api -X DELETE repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels/<label>` /
`gh api -X POST repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels -f 'labels[]=<label>'`), shi ma kai tsaye.

## Saka janitor (sau ɗaya, a kan na’urar)

```bash
scp scripts/ops/runner-janitor.sh root@192.168.0.113:/opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh
ssh root@192.168.0.113 'chmod +x /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh; apt-get install -y lsof'
# cron (root): kowane minti 30, a rubuta log zuwa /var/log/runner-janitor.log
*/30 * * * * MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6 /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh >> /var/log/runner-janitor.log 2>&1
```

Ana buƙatar `lsof`: janitor yana tabbatar da cewa babu abin da ke amfani da wata hanya ta hanyar ɗaukar hoto guda na fayilolin da suke buɗe kafin ya cire ta, kuma idan babu wannan kayan aikin ba ya cire komai, sannan ya faɗi hakan
(exit 1). Da farko, gwada kowane canji da `--dry-run` — yana buga ainihin abin da zai
yi ba tare da taɓa komai ba.

Abin da yake yi a kowane gudu: yana share ragowar namu (`runner-*`, `omniroute-*`,
`next-build*`, `e2e-build.tar.gz`) bayan **awa 3 a kan tmpfs** da awa 24 a kan faifan
`_work/_temp`; yana kashe `next-build` da ya wuce minti 75 (babu wani job da ke gudana na tsawon haka — a ranar
2026-08-27 wani ya gudana na minti 70 bayan GitHub ya bayyana cewa job ɗinsa ya ɓace); yana cire
checkouts da suka kai awa 48 na runners waɗanda unit ɗinsu ya **tsaya**; yana aika faɗakarwa idan amfanin faifai ya kai ≥ 85 %, memory PSI
`full/avg60` ya kai ≥ 10 %, da kuma idan listeners sun fi `MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS` yawa (tare da
rarrabuwar omniroute/wasu). Exit 1 = ana buƙatar kulawa; karanta log ɗin.

Dokar minti 75 ba ta gano build wanda GitHub ya riga ya yi watsi da job ɗinsa. Lokacin da
`Runner.Listener` ba zai iya sabunta job ɗinsa ba, `_diag/Runner_*.log` yana nuna
`POST request to …/renewjob timed out after 100 seconds`, API yana nuna cewa job ɗin ya `completed`
(ya gaza) kimanin minti goma bayan sabuntawa na ƙarshe da ya yi nasara, kuma `next-build` yana ci gaba da gudana
ba tare da wani job da aka haɗa da shi ba (2026-10-18: ginin #17066 ya gaza da ƙarfe 19:43, amma build ɗinsa har yanzu
yana riƙe da 40 GB bayan minti biyar). Tabbatar da ref a cikin `_diag/Worker_*.log` mafi sabo, sannan
aika SIGTERM ga bishiyar build kawai ta amfani da PID — `next-build`, `npm run build`, `build-next-isolated.mjs` da
workers na `_work`. Kada a taɓa `Runner.Listener`, `RunnerService.js` ko `Runner.Worker`: worker ɗin
yana bayar da rahoton cewa an soke job ɗin, kuma listener ɗin yana komawa online da kansa.

## Rukunan runner: KillMode

Tsohon saitin runner na `KillMode=process` yana barin `Runner.Worker → npm → next-build`
a raye idan aka dakatar ko aka sake kunna wani rukuni — build marar mai kula yana ci gaba da cin RAM da
CPU ba tare da wani job da ke haɗe da shi ba. Kowane rukunin OmniRoute yana da drop-in
(`/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.diegosouzapw-OmniRoute.<name>.service.d/10-killmode.conf`)
mai `KillMode=mixed`: SIGTERM zuwa listener da farko, sannan SIGKILL zuwa dukan cgroup a
`TimeoutStop`. Zai fara aiki a sake kunnawa na gaba na rukunin — sake kunna **runner ɗaya a
kowane lokaci, sai lokacin da ba ya aiki**, tare da binciken rashin aiki da sake kunnawar a cikin umarni guda.

## Dokokin aiki

- **Ginin Turbopack guda ɗaya kawai a lokaci guda — ana tilasta wannan ta hanyar label.** Duk aikin da ke gudanar da cikakken ginin Turbopack
  `next build` yana nufar `[self-hosted, omni-build]`, kuma **`omniroute-113-5`** kaɗai ne ke ɗauke da wannan
  label: `ci.yml` `Build`, `npm-publish.yml` `publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`,
  ginin a11y na dare (#16853) da kuma `docker-publish.yml` **amd64** (webpack a cikin BuildKit —
  Turbopack ya gamu da panic a can, gudu na 33253576569). Bangaren Docker na arm64 yana ci gaba da kasancewa a `ubuntu-24.04-arm`.
  `docker-publish` amd64 yana raba rukunin concurrency na `heavy-build-main` da `ci.yml` `Build`
  (`cancel-in-progress: false`). Dole ne Docker Engine ya kasance a kan `omniroute-113-5`. Gurbin Turbopack
  na biyu ba zai iya shiga cikin 78 GB ba (2 × 33–37 GB tare da sauran abubuwan da ke kan na’urar); gurbi na biyu yana da aminci ne kawai
  ga gine-ginen webpack.
- **Gine-ginen pull-request suna gudana a hosted (2026-10-18).** `ci.yml` `Build` yana isa `omni-build` ne kawai don
  `push` da `workflow_dispatch`; kowane `pull_request` (daga asalin repo ko fork) da `merge_group` Build
  yana gudana a kan `ubuntu-latest` tare da webpack. Lokacin da aka tura kowane PR daga asalin repo zuwa gurbin, wani Turbopack
  Build yana riƙe shi na mintuna 29–58, Builds guda 18 suka yi layi har zuwa awa 18, kuma push Build na
  layin fitarwa da kansa ya sami gurbin sau ɗaya ne kawai cikin ayyuka 40. A hosted, wannan PR Build ɗin yana ɗaukar mintuna 8–12
  tare da sauran ayyukan PR ɗin. Turbopack, wato bundler da `npm-publish` ke fitarwa, ana ci gaba da gwada shi a layin fitarwa ta hanyar
  ginin a11y na dare na default branch (04:41 UTC), ta kowane push ko dispatch Build da
  ya isa gurbin, da kuma ta `npm-publish` da kansa. `tests/unit/build/heavy-build-queue.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da wannan ƙofar mai sharuɗɗa huɗu.
- **Kada a taɓa rage girman gurbin Turbopack ƙasa da buƙatarsa da aka auna.** A 2026-10-18 da ƙarfe 18:06 an
  rage gurbin daga 56G/60G zuwa 40G/44G domin samar wa `omni-release` sarari. Matakin compile ya tsaya a
  `MemoryHigh` tsawon duk gudunsa (memory PSI `full` 30–43 %), kuma `Runner.Listener`, wanda
  ke raba cgroup ɗin, bai iya sabunta aikinsa ba. An maido da saitin mai shi na 56G/60G daga 2026-10-17 a
  wannan rana. Idan an rage ƙasa da wannan ƙimar, ginin ba ya faduwa; yana ta faman amfani da albarkatu ba tare da ci gaba ba, kuma aikin yana ɓacewa.
- **Release-Green yana da nasa gurbin (2026-10-18).** Kafin wannan, dukkan ingantattun tantancewar Release-Green suna gudana
  a kan `omni-build`: a kowane push (~10/awa, an soke 97 daga cikin gudu 100 na push a tsakiyar aiki cikin rana ɗaya)
  da cikakkun sweeps guda uku masu ɗaukar kusan awa 2 a rana (sweep na 05:29 ya jira awa 6 don samun gurbin) — an ɗora wa gurbin gini
  guda ɗaya ayyuka fiye da ƙarfinsa sau da yawa, kuma PR Builds daga asalin repo sun jira na tsawon awanni.
  `release-green` da `main-green` yanzu suna nufar `omni-release` (`omniroute-113-6` kaɗai) tare da
  `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK=0`, kuma ba a sake soke gudun da ya fara saboda wani sabon push (amma
  gurbin da ke jira har yanzu yana tattara cunkoson ya rage shi). `tests/unit/build/runner-capacity-routing.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da routing, sauyawa zuwa webpack, da kuma cewa babu wani abu a `omni-release` da yake gini da Turbopack.
- **Rukunin ayyuka masu sauƙi: `omni-light`.** `omniroute-113` da `omniroute-113-2` suna gudanar da ayyukan dare da ke buƙatar
  `next build` na backend kawai (~5–6 GB; webpack) da `quality.yml` `lint-guard`. `omniroute-113`
  yana da `MemoryHigh=4G` har zuwa 2026-10-18 — ginin promptfoo ya shafe mintuna 54 yana ta kwato memory
  a can; 8 GB yana kawar da throttling.
- **An rage yawan listeners da gangan.** An kashe units huɗu na OmniRoute a 2026-08-29 — kowane ƙarin
  listener wani mai amfani ne daban ga kasafin memory da ke sama. Matsakaicin iyakar janitor ita ce 6 (`MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6` a cikin cron): yana ƙirga
  kowane `Runner.Listener` da ke kan na’urar, kuma OmniHeuris + OmniMind suna ƙara biyu a kan namu guda huɗu.
- **Kada a taɓa tsaftace `/tmp` ko `_work` da hannu yayin da kowane runner yake aiki.** Yin
  dubawa-sannan-sharewa tare da tazara tsakanin matakan biyu shi ne yadda wani Build job mai gudana ya rasa
  `_work` ɗinsa a 2026-08-27. Janitor yana yin dubawa da cirewa a mataki guda;
  a bar shi ya yi.
- Tsayar da runner a tsakiyar aiki yana soke aikin (an gani kai tsaye): a yi `systemctl stop` ne kawai
  lokacin da listener ɗinsa ba shi da ɗan `Runner.Worker` — kuma a yi hakan cikin umarni guda.
- Kada workflows su ajiye artefacts a cikin `/tmp` (RAM ne). A sauke su zuwa
  `$RUNNER_TEMP` (a kan disk, na kowane runner) — artefact na `next-build` mai girman 1.3 GB ya ɗauki mintuna 27–32
  kafin ya sauka a kan tmpfs, amma mintuna 2 kawai wajen upload daga disk.
- VPS na `.15` don homologation kawai ne — ba ya taɓa gudanar da CI runners.
